01 / ABOUT & INTERESTS

Research background

I am a fully funded M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Bilkent University and a graduate researcher at NeuraVision Lab. My work explores robust and efficient learning methods for visual problems, from uncertainty quantification and nucleus segmentation to trajectory prediction and optimization.

Before Bilkent, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering at the University of Tabriz, where I worked on medical image analysis and led research on pose-guided sign-language video generation.
